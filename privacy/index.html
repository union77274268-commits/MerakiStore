Who we are

Meraki Stationery & Toys is a shop at Shop No. LG-06, Below HDFC Bank, Kalwar Road, Jaipur 302012, India. You can reach us on +91 83024 16181 or at the shop. This website and app are how you browse and order from us.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and optional mobile number. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We do not get your Google password or access to your Google account.
  • Orders and delivery: items you order, delivery address, optional map pin (latitude and longitude), delivery instructions, payment method and order status.
  • Your cart, wishlist, rewards and "notify me" requests.
  • Notification settings: if you turn on notifications, a device token so we can send push notifications. We also store your choices for order emails and offer emails.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders and show your order history.
  • To send order and delivery updates, back-in-stock alerts and, if you opted in, offers by notification or email.
  • To keep your account secure and to prevent misuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who helps us run the service

We use trusted providers to operate the app: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (website hosting), an email delivery provider (to send emails), Google (if you choose Google sign-in), and OpenStreetMap services (map display and address search when you pin a location). They process data only to provide their service to us.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications or emails off at any time in Account → Settings. Every promotional email also has an unsubscribe link.
  • Edit your details or delivery addresses in the app.
  • Delete your account in Account → Delete account. This removes your account and personal data from the app. We may keep basic order records where we need them for accounting or legal reasons.

Keeping data safe

Data is stored with industry-standard providers and access is restricted. No online service can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable care.

Children

Our shop sells things children love, but accounts and orders are meant for adults. Children should use the app with a parent or guardian.
